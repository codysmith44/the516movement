# the516movement.com: site notes (fetched 2026-10-13)
**Purpose:** Site for M516, Inc. (The 516 Movement), a Christ-centered traveling ministry run by evangelist Ryan Young (Elk City, OK), based on Matthew 5:16. It takes event/speaking bookings and donations. 501(c)3.
**Current stack:** WordPress + iThemes "Builder" theme (child "the-516-movement"), Gravity Forms (booking/contact), GiveWP + Stripe (donations). The old layout dates from 2015.

## Navigation
Home · M516 (About, Our Values, About Ryan) · Resources (Videos, The Answer, Armor Up, Newsletter) · Booking · Donate (The Marathon) · Contact

## Key copy
- Home: "M516 is a Christ-centered ministry designed to reach the corners of the earth with the redeeming message of the Gospel for all people." CTAs: Learn More About M516 / Book Your Next Event.
- About: Matthew 5:16 life, "live for revival", "point people to the Cross", "Let the movement begin!"
- Our Values: Ministry / Missions / Movement definitions + Matt 5:16.
- About Ryan: wife Karistin, kids Annslee & Cross; 20 yrs student ministry; missions; revival.
- The Answer: Gospel presentation (Gen 1:27, John 10:10, Rom 3:23, 6:23, 5:8, Eph 2:8-9, John 14:6, Rom 10:9) + salvation prayer.
- Armor Up: daily Ephesians 6 armor prayer.
- Booking: intro + long Gravity Forms questionnaire.
- Donate: Pray / Share / Give; check to PO Box; GiveWP form.
- The Marathon: 2025 10-year fundraiser; commemorative coins; 8 partnership tiers ($100 to $30,000+).
- Contact: phone, address, email, contact form.
